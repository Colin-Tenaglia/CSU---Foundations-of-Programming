Preparer Training — Lessons 1 through 18

Publication first, then the Drake screen, then where it lands on the return. One lesson per sitting. Do not skip a gate.

Before you start

Publications are cited by lesson name and tab letter, not page number. Pub 4491 renumbers every year; the lesson titles do not. Confirm the Pub 4012 tab letters against your printed copy the first time you use one.

Drake screen codes come from the Drake KB Federal 1040 Screen List, updated 24 March 2026. Type a code into the selector box at the bottom of any data entry screen. Press F1 on an open screen for that screen's own help.

Citations are links. Every publication and IRS form named in a Read line, a What-to-look-for item, a Where-it-lands line or a screen table is clickable and goes to the authority itself — publications to the current PDF, forms to their IRS About page, which carries the form, its instructions and recent developments together. That is the deeper dive: click the form rather than searching for it.

Every dollar figure lives in the Figures & Lines Sheet, not in these lessons. Where to verify a figure shows how to check any of them at source.

Whose job is which. These lessons cover the return from intake through filing. Inside a firm, the preparer who signs owns the e-file authorization, the PIN screen, the EF selections and the transmission, and carries the Circular 230 and §6695 exposure that goes with signing — so if you are learning inside a firm, expect to hand off at that line, with a complete reviewed return and every EF message accounted for. If you are preparing your own return there is no handoff and the whole sequence is yours. Lesson 12 teaches all of it either way, because you cannot review a step competently that you have never been shown.

New for tax year 2025: Schedule 1-A on screen 1A — tips, overtime, car loan interest, senior deduction. Full treatment in Part 2, Lesson 13A.

Turn these on before Lesson 1

  • Setup > Options > Data Entry — W-2 wage and withholding verification. Forces re-entry of boxes 1, 2, 16 and 17; mismatches throw EF messages 5477, 5478, 5501, 5503.
  • Setup > Options > Data Entry — Data Entry toolbar and grid entry on DIV, INT, 4562, dependents.
  • Setup > Options — Always show tax computation worksheet and Always show reason for no EIC.

Use the practice returns that ship with the software. Do not use live client data for training.
