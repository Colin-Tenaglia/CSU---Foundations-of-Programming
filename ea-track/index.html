Publication first, then the Drake screen, then where it lands on the return. Every publication and form named below is a link to the authority itself.

How to use this

Reference order in every lesson: publication first, then the return, then the Drake screen. That order is deliberate. Someone who learns the software before the return can fill a screen correctly and still put the result in the wrong place.

Citations are links. Every publication and IRS form named in a Read line, a What-to-look-for item, a Where-it-lands line or a screen table is clickable. Publications go to the current PDF; forms go to their IRS About page, which carries the form, its instructions and recent developments together.

Publications are cited by lesson name and tab letter, not page number. Pub 4491 renumbers every year; the lesson titles do not. Confirm the Pub 4012 tab letters against your printed copy the first time you use one.

Drake screen codes come from the Drake KB Federal 1040 Screen List. Type a code into the selector box at the bottom of any data entry screen. Press F1 on an open screen for that screen's own help, which answers for the program year actually installed. Codes marked † are not yet confirmed against a Drake source.

No amounts appear in the lessons. Every cap, floor, threshold and phaseout lives in the Figures & Lines Sheet on the second tab, and Where to verify a figure shows how to check any of them at source.

Whose job is which. These lessons cover the return from intake through filing. Inside a firm, the preparer who signs owns the e-file authorization, the PIN screen, the EF selections and the transmission, and carries the Circular 230 and §6695 exposure that goes with signing — so if you are learning inside a firm, expect to hand off at that line. Preparing your own return, the whole sequence is yours.

Gates are real. They are competency checks, not review sections, and the one after Lesson 12 is marked hard because someone who has never completed a return has nothing to attach Ring A to.

Turn these on before Lesson 1

  • Setup > Options > Data Entry — W-2 wage and withholding verification. Forces re-entry of boxes 1, 2, 16 and 17; mismatches throw EF messages before transmission.
  • Setup > Options > Data Entry — Data Entry toolbar and grid entry on DIV, INT, 4562, dependents.
  • Setup > Options — Always show tax computation worksheet and Always show reason for no EIC.
  • Setup > Firm, Preparer, ERO — PTIN, EFIN and firm information. A missing or wrong PTIN is a preparer penalty issue, not a formatting issue.

Use the practice returns that ship with the software. Do not use live client data for training.

New for tax year 2025: Schedule 1-A on screen 1A — tips, overtime, car loan interest, senior deduction. Full treatment in Lesson 13A on the second tab.
